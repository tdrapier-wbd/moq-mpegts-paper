# Cross-check: `moq-dev` and `moq2ts`

**State: register opened. Answers by code at `moq-dev` `main` `cd560bde5e` and `moq2ts` `main` `5ea1c0b60`, and three laptop checks run; two shared properties found, one drafted for `moq-dev` and one by its design.**

`moq-dev` and OpenMOQ's `moq2ts` both carry MPEG-TS over MoQ. `moq-dev` does it in Rust, on `moq-lite` and MoQT, with its own catalog beside MSF. `moq2ts` does it in C++, publishing on `moqxr` and receiving on `moq5`, on MoQT under MSF and MSFTS. A defect found or fixed in one is a hypothesis about the other. This file holds each such item, the question it puts to the other implementation, the answer with its evidence, and what follows. Whether the two exchange media is interop, which is [T11](test-11-interop.md)'s.

## The loop

1. **Find.** [`cross-audit.sh`](scripts/cross-audit.sh) lists every `moq2ts` issue and pull request updated since a date, and every `moq-dev` `main` commit since a given one that touches its TS path (`moq-mux`'s TS container and catalog, `moq-srt`, `moq-msf`). It marks each `new` unless this file cites it. Our own `moq-dev` posts reach it through the [upstream ledger](upstream-contributions.md). It runs in each session's tail beside the upstream and quest audits, and `STATUS.md` keeps the date and commit it last ran from.
2. **Triage.** An item gets a row only if it names a property the other implementation could share: a clause of MOQT, MSF or MSFTS, a carriage rule, a client's behaviour. Build, packaging and UI items get none.
3. **Answer** with the cheapest evidence that settles the question, at a named commit: _code_ (read), _check_ (a laptop command, below) or _test_ (cells in a `test-*.md`, criteria first). A row stays _open_ until one has run.
4. **Act.** A gap with evidence becomes a draft in `lab/upstream/`, posted only with the author's go-ahead, citing the other implementation's fix where there is one. Where `moq2ts`'s contributors already have an open issue on the point, our evidence goes there as a comment, not as a new issue.
5. **Close** a row when the answer is "not shared", or when the counterpart's fix has merged and been verified. A closed row moves to the list below it; an item we posted keeps its outcome in the ledger.

**The connect-error check** ([`xc-connect-errors.sh`](scripts/xc-connect-errors.sh), loopback, `cd560bde5e`). Each case is one `moq import ts` under a 30 s timeout. A case shows #45's behaviour if the client is still dialling at 30 s after at least three attempts; `v6` passes if it connects as the control does. The control and `v6` connected; `dns`, `cert` and `version` were still dialling at 30 s, after 10, 10 and 9 attempts.

**The audio-start check** ([`xc-audio-start.sh`](scripts/xc-audio-start.sh), loopback, `cd560bde5e`). The clip's MP2 audio is remuxed alone at 228 kb/s, with the PCR on its PID, and fed at its own rate; the control keeps the 500 random access indicators the remux writes, the treatment has none. Each lane, the demultiplexed import and `--passthrough`, is one run per fixture: a subscriber waits, then the import starts. [#41](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/issues/41)'s behaviour is shared if the first audio PES reaches the subscriber more than 10 s after the import starts, the bound [#42](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/pull/42) adopts. It came at 3.1 s on the import with and without indicators, and on `--passthrough` at 1.2 s with them and 2.3 s without, export's 1 s delay included. A first run timed the first output byte, which the export's tables can precede; the measure was tightened after it, the bar unchanged, and its readings agree within 0.05 s.

**The PAT-change check** ([`xc-pat-change.sh`](scripts/xc-pat-change.sh), loopback). The clip runs 20 s as one programme, then its PAT gains a second, 228 kb/s of the clip's own audio on new PIDs. It is fed paced, over UDP, to `moq import ts` under a supervisor that restarts it with the same arguments whenever it exits, and a subscriber relaunched in the same way records each video PES it receives. [#33](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/issues/33)'s behaviour, a track ended by a PAT change and not replaced, is shared if no video reaches the subscriber in the 10 s after the second programme appears. The control, `--program 1`, should carry through. A case is void unless video reaches the subscriber before the change: a first run was, its UDP input bound wrongly, and is set aside. Without `--program` the import exited 1 at the change (_transport stream carries 2 programs_), and all 65 restarts were refused alike: no video reached the subscriber from 1.4 s after the change to the run's end, 40 s on. With `--program 1` one import ran through and delivered 1,345 video PES after the change, against the source's 1,342.

## Both ways

**MSFTS carriage.** `moq-dev`'s MSF reader skips any track packaged other than LOC, CMAF or legacy, and its MSF types have no `mpeg2ts` packaging. Its whole multiplex appears only in its own catalog's `m2ts` section. Neither implementation therefore finds the other's TS through MSF (code). That is [moq-dev#3731](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/issues/3731)'s question, open. Its quest, `msfts-convergence`, was pruned on 2026-11-09 for want of a named consumer, to be re-planned when one asks; `moq2ts`'s carriage is one, which a comment on #3731 could name.

## Checked, not shared

- `moq2ts` [#30](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/issues/30) and [#49](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/issues/49) ([#53](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/pull/53)), groups when the source never marks random access, and audio-only programs. `moq-dev`'s passthrough starts a group at the first PCR one second into a group without a random access point, and groups an audio-only program on its clock PID. It also caps a group at 16 MB, at 4,096 objects, or at half what the relay caches of one group (code).
- [#32](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/issues/32), reconnecting after the relay drops: `moq-dev` reconnects with backoff and follows a GOAWAY (code).
- [#29](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/issues/29), the track role, and [#31](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/issues/31), [#43](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/issues/43) and [#48](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/issues/48) ([#51](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/pull/51)), the timeline track. `moq-dev`'s MSF catalog takes each role from the rendition, video or audio, and declares no timeline track (code).
- [#17](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/pull/17) and [#18](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/pull/18), each program of a multiplex as its own track: `moq import ts --program all` publishes each program as its own broadcast.
- [#12](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/pull/12), an SRT listener: `moq-srt` listens and dials out (code).
- [#47](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/issues/47) ([#52](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/pull/52)), a PCR discontinuity inside a group after a restart. `moq-dev`'s passthrough starts a group at each flagged forward PCR discontinuity and ends the import at any step back, so no group holds one. An SRT loss is no group boundary there: `moq-srt` hands each payload straight to the demultiplexing importer, with no passthrough and no loss marker (code).
- #41, a low-rate source without random access indicators: `moq-dev` starts it within about 3 s on both lanes, the passthrough's one-second PCR bound on a group taking the indicator's place (check above).
- [#21](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/issues/21), a bracketed IPv6 endpoint: `moq` connects to `https://[::1]:…` (check above).
- [#20](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/issues/20) and [#19](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/issues/19). `moq-dev` verifies the relay's certificate unless told not to, and its relay accepts MoQT drafts 18, 20 and 22.
- `moq-dev` [#8383](https://github.com/moq-dev/moq/issues/8383), a padded output's back-fill after a block: `moq2ts`'s receiver writes the packets it receives, neither paced nor padded (its README).

Interop, not a shared property: [#54](https://github.com/openmoq/moq2ts/pull/54) sends a relay authorization token at setup, tested against moqx. Whether `moq-relay` admits it is [T53](test-53-token-interop.md)'s question.
